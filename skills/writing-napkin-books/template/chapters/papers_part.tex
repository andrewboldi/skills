\part{The Papers}\label{part:papers}
\addchap{The papers in this volume}
\label{ch:papers}

The combined edition of this book continues, after this table, with the papers themselves: the \npapers{} arXiv
papers cited in the book, in the order of the table, joined into one file. Each is the version that arXiv served on
\fillin{the download date}. The last column gives the page of the combined file on which a paper starts, counted as
the PDF viewer counts, from the title page; click a title or a page number to jump there, or use the bookmarks,
where the papers are listed under \emph{The Papers}. The pages of paper~$n$ are labeled P$n$.1, P$n$.2, \dots{} in
the viewer. Links inside a paper (to its own bibliography, sections and equations) work as in the original.

Books, journal articles, web pages and other sources that are not on arXiv are not included; they are listed at the
end of this part. Where a card in the book describes a paper, the table says on which page; the stars give the
reading tier (three stars: essential).
